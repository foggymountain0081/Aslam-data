What the comparison found

  • Sametham register: 204 schools. 54 of them are in the 56 executive areas (17 already visited, 37 not yet). The other 150 are in areas the team has not visited, so they are left out.
  • Visited schools: 19 of the 63 visited entries match 17 Sametham schools (the High School and Higher Secondary sections of Kumbala and Neerchal are logged separately). The other 44 are not in this Sametham extract: mostly Hosdurg/Kanhangad-side schools, private schools and LP/UP schools it does not cover.
  • New pending schools: comparing both files found 22 schools in the executive areas that the earlier visit-log report did not list as pending (marked new).
  • Corrections: 4 schools the visit-log report listed as pending were actually visited already: A. S. B. S. Ichlampady (same phone as visited Ichlampady Senior Basic School); A. J. B. S. Puthige (same as visited Junior Basic School, Puthige); G. H. S. S. Paivalike Nagar (same as visited GHSS School, Paivalike); M P International School (same as visited MP International School, Shiribagilu). The report also listed 5 schools twice under different areas; each is counted once here.
  • Free passes: neither file has any free-pass figure, so this count cannot be given. Share the free-pass log and it can be added.
  • Mobile numbers: a number counts as mobile when it is 10 digits starting with 6–9. Most Sametham numbers are school landlines (04994 / 04998).
