Town schools that Deepika listed without a sub-district were placed by town. The State Syllabus and Deepika lists overlap almost fully, so each school is counted once (348 unique directory schools after adding CBSE/ICSE and removing duplicates).
  3. Visited = the school appears in the executive visit log. Each visit row was matched to the directory by hand (school type + locality, spelling variants such as Thariyod/Thariode, Kunjihome/Kunhome, Valad/Valat). Where a school was contacted more than once (e.g. HS and HSS sections), it is counted once.
  4. Anganwadis, colleges, ITIs, pre-schools and private schools that are not in any directory are listed per area under "Other institutions visited". Rows with no place were given the area of the other schools visited the same day.
  5. 2nd visit month: taken from the "Second Visit" column of the visit log. Where that column is blank, the month is derived as one month before the earliest tour month mentioned in the latest remark, and is marked with *.
  6. Chance of confirmation: HIGH = tour month given in the latest call and the contact is reachable, or the school used the free stay pass and is interested; MEDIUM = details shared / follow-up needed / tour month mentioned earlier but not reachable on the latest call; LOW = not interested, wrong or non-existent number, or never reached.
  7. "No mobile number" means no 10-digit mobile number in any source; only an office landline (04935/04936) or no number at all is available, so these schools need a direct first visit.
  8. Executive name: every named row in the executive visited data is "Noufal"; the name Aslam (and "Azlam") appears only in the free stay pass feedback, for two schools outside Wayanad. The Wayanad visit log is therefore treated as the covered area for this report.
