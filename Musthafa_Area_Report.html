5. How this was checked

• A school is treated as the same school when the phone number matches, or when the school name and place match after removing spelling differences (e.g. “G L P School, Pathanapuram” = “G.L.P.S. Pathanapuram”; Nilamboor = Nilambur; Areakod = Areekode).

• 2nd visit: Yes when Musthafa visited the same school again (same phone number on a later date or marked “Second visit”). The date of the repeat visit is shown. Follow-up / tour month is taken from the “Follow-up”, “second visit” and “Tour Time Month” columns of his sheet.

• Free pass: “Used” = school appears in the Free Pass data with a park-visit date. “Given, not used yet” = his remarks say a free pass was given but no park visit is recorded.

• Total schools in an area counts only schools whose name or address contains that place name in Deepika's data or the Malappuram school list, so it may be lower than the real number of schools there.

• Rows where the Place column was blank are grouped under “Place not mentioned”.

• Some names are written very differently in each list (abbreviations, short names); a few real matches may show as “No”, so please check important ones by phone number.
