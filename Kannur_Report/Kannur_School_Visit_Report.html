How this report was prepared

  1. Executive visit log (Biju Thomas & Muhammed, 14.08.2026 – 23.9.2026): 145 schools/institutions visited in Kannur. Each school is counted once; its contact numbers and all call remarks are merged.
  2. Main areas follow the Kerala assembly-constituency grouping used in the State-Syllabus school list. Only the 5 areas the executives actually covered are included: Taliparamba, Irikkur (Alakode–Naduvil hill belt), Payyannur, Kannur Town and Azhikode.
  3. Total schools in an area = schools already visited + schools not yet visited from the State-Syllabus list (Govt/Aided/Unaided), the CBSE/ICSE Kannur list and Deepika's school data. Where the same school appears in more than one list it is counted once.
  4. Deepika's data = the 'School kannur' agency list (ID / Pwd / Agency code / Status 'N-V'). Every visited school was checked by name and phone number against this list ('In Deepika data?' column). The Deepika mobile-number sheet shows each Deepika school in the visited areas that has a mobile number, and whether an executive has visited it.
  5. 2nd-visit month = the tour month the school gave on the call. 'Follow-up call' = interested/undecided but no month given. 'Re-try contact' = the call did not connect or the number was wrong.
  6. Confirmation probability: High = tour month given and trip being planned; Medium = month given but trip not yet fixed, or details shared / call back requested; Low = not interested, no trip this year, or distance issue; Not reached = call not connected or wrong number; No remarks yet = visited but no call outcome was written down.
  7. Free-stay-pass feedback: 82 feedback entries were checked (Interested 38, Neutral 27, Not interested 2, blank 15). None of them match a Kannur school by name or phone number, so the feedback column says 'No free-pass feedback on record' for every Kannur school.
  8. Mobile number = a 10-digit number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9. Numbers such as 0460-xxxxxxx are landlines. 469 Deepika entries fall outside the visited areas (Kasaragod, Iritty, Thalassery, Karnataka, etc.) and are not shown.
