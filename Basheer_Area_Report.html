How to read

  • Sources: Basheer's visit log (17 pages), Kozhikode School Data (1,277 schools with assembly constituency), Deepika's Kozhikode data (1,283 schools with block and phone), the CBSE/ICSE list (Kozhikode part: 99 CBSE and 4 ICSE), and the free stay-pass feedback sheet (all executives).
  • Main area is the assembly constituency. Each visit is placed by the matched directory school if there is one, otherwise by its place name. If the place name is missing or unknown, it takes the area of the other visits on the same day.
  • Matching visits to the directory: Matched means the same phone number, or a strong match on place, school type and name. Probable means the place and type agree but the name is generic (for example "A U P School"). Pre-primary schools, anganwadis, colleges, ITIs and buds schools are not in the directories, so they show as "Not found in directory lists".
  • In Deepika? is Yes when the matched school appears in Deepika's list.
  • Pending first visit means a directory school in the area with no matching visit in Basheer's log and no free-pass feedback.
  • Mobile vs landline: 10-digit numbers starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9 count as mobiles. Numbers like 496xxxxxxx or 495xxxxxxx are STD landlines.
  • Confirmation probability (my estimate): Confirmed 90% = "tour confirmed", a date given, or "will be coming". High 70–75% = free-pass feedback says "Interested". Medium 50–55% = a tour month was given, details were shared, "will confirm", or a second visit is planned. Low 20–25% = busy or call not answered. Very Low 5% = not interested, wrong or invalid number, or a tour already booked elsewhere.
