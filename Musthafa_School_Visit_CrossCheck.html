How the check was done

  • Every row of Musthafa's log was compared with the other four files by phone number, by school name (including short forms such as GLPS, AMLPS, CKHS, SVLPS, MCUPS) and by place.   • Free pass: Used = the school appears in the free-pass visit data or tracker, or the log says the pass was used; Booked = a free-pass visit date is booked; Given = the log says the pass was given/shared but there is no record of use.
